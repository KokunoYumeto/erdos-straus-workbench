# Contributing

Contributions are welcome from humans, proof assistants, computer algebra systems, and AI-assisted investigations. The unit of contribution is a bounded, checkable mathematical claim—not a reputation judgment or an endpoint implication.

Useful contributions include:

- exact literature or historical source records;
- corrections to definitions, hypotheses, maps, coordinates, or citations;
- reproducible computations and independent replays;
- bounded lemmas, counterexamples, obstructions, or failed approaches;
- Lean formal acceptance reviews;
- separate reviews that the formal statement matches the intended mathematics.

Every contribution should state objects, domains, codomains, maps, exceptional cases, hypotheses, dependencies, and nonclaims. Label conjectures and analogies as such. A calculation that passes its own checker remains `CLAIMED_UNVERIFIED` until independently reviewed.

Do not upload raw transcripts, prompt histories, private communications, personal context, credentials, copyrighted bulk text, unlicensed paper source, or unrelated workspace material. Cite books and papers; do not copy them into the repository unless redistribution rights are clear.
