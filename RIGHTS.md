# Rights and publication boundary

To the extent their contributors have authority to do so, Commons-originated material intentionally submitted to this repository is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. This includes the workbench metadata and workflow records, the independently written mod-107 TeX/PDF note, the Python and Lean certificates, and the typed claim/dependency/morphism/crosswalk records. The top-level `LICENSE` gives the controlling repository notice.

That dedication does **not** sweep in:

- the underlying Erdős–Straus literature;
- the Zenodo archive or inherited Clankers manuscripts;
- third-party articles, books, scans, source TeX, figures, datasets, or software;
- Reddit posts or unpublished notes;
- private transcripts, correspondence, prompts, or local research context.

Third-party materials retain their own rights and licenses. A public URL is not by itself a reuse license. This repository cites or records metadata for rights-uncertain sources instead of redistributing their bytes.

The mod-107 note and certificates are published for inspection, reuse, and collaborative review under that dedication. Their factual dependence on third-party public locators does not relicense those sources. No blanket license is asserted over inherited project content, and no historical priority claim is made. Contributors must identify the rights basis for every uploaded artifact.
