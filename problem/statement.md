# Canonical Erdős–Straus problem statement

For every integer \(n\ge 2\), determine whether there exist positive integers \(x,y,z\) satisfying

\[
\frac{4}{n}=\frac1x+\frac1y+\frac1z.
\]

This workbench calls the universal existence assertion the **Erdős–Straus conjecture**.

The variables are ordered only for reporting: because the equation is symmetric in \(x,y,z\), a certificate may be normalized to \(x\le y\le z\) without changing existence. Unless a packet explicitly narrows its domain, \(n\) ranges over all integers at least two. Prime-only reductions, residue classes, divisor-shell coordinates, density statements, and finite computations are variants or intermediate results, not alternative endpoint statements.

This is newly written Mathematics Commons wording. It does not copy a historical source statement and makes no assertion of novelty, priority, or solution.
